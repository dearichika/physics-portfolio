Scope of Physics → The study of everything

Physics → The science of matter and energy and their interaction with each other

Mechanics → involves motion / everything that moves

Thermodynamics → heat transfer

  • There is no such thing as coldness / absence of heat
  • Humidity → moisture of the air
  • Water absorbs heat

  • Voltage → flow of electricity
  • Ampere → amount of electricity
  • Ohm → resistance of electricity
Speed

Speed is how fast something moves.

Formula:

v = d / t

Displacement
  • Distance + direction
  • Distance between starting and stopping point
Velocity
  • Speed + direction
  • Total distance covered in a period of time with direction

Formula:

v = displacement / time

Acceleration

Change in velocity over time

Formula:

a = (vf - vi) / t

3 Scenarios:

  1. Constant speed, change in direction
  2. Constant direction, change in speed
  3. Change in both direction and speed
Free Fall Motion
  • Gravity is the only force acting on the object
  • g = -9.8m/s²
Newton's Laws of Motion

1. Law of Inertia

An object remains at rest unless acted upon by force

2. Law of Acceleration

F = ma

3. Law of Interaction

Every action has an equal and opposite reaction

Density & Specific Gravity

Density Formula:

ρ = m / V

Specific Gravity Formula:

Archimedes Principle
  • Positive buoyancy → floats
  • Negative buoyancy → sinks
  • Neutral buoyancy → sideways movement

Fb = ρgV

Static Electricity

Build-up of electric charge on the surface of an object

Rules of Electric Charge

  1. Like charges repel
  2. Opposite charges attract
  3. Charge is conserved
Coulomb's Law

F = (Kq1q2) / r²

Electric Field
  • Field lines never intersect
  • Denser lines = stronger field
  • Positive arrows point outward
  • Negative arrows point inward

Thermal Expansion

Expansion occurs when heat is present

Formula:

ΔL = αLiΔT

  • ΔL = change in length
  • α = coefficient of expansion
  • ΔT = change in temperature
  • Li = original length

ΔV = βViΔT

Heat Capacity & Specific Heat

Heat Capacity

  • The amount of energy it can withstand
  • Each material has different capacities

Specific Heat

  • The amount of calories needed to raise the temperature of 1 gram of substance by 1°C

Formula:

Q = mCΔT
